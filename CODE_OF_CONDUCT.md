# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DesktopKeeper is a community project. We want participation in its issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces to be respectful and
welcoming, regardless of a person's background, identity, experience, or
technical skill.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and behavior, not a person's worth or identity.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Make room for different experiences and communication styles.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information
  without their permission.
- Follow the project contribution guidance and the rules of the platforms
  where project discussions take place.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive
behavior after a reasonable request to stop may also be addressed.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests,
reviews, and project-related discussions. It also applies when someone is
representing the project in another space.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that conflicts with this code, contact
the project maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sap112233). Please do not
report a sensitive conduct concern in a public issue. Include links or other
context that will help the maintainer understand what happened, and avoid
sharing unrelated private information.

The maintainer will review reports as capacity allows, respect the privacy of
the people involved where possible, and decide on an appropriate response.
Responses may include a reminder of expectations, a warning, removal of
content, or restriction from project spaces. There is no guaranteed response
time. GitHub's platform reporting tools are also available for conduct that
violates GitHub's rules.

This code is maintained by the project maintainer and may be updated as the
project and its community grow.
